Built with Jordan Senko, Shawn Lee and Darren Law Yan Lun, StretchGoals won first place at MRuHacks 2026. The desktop posture coach calibrates to how you sit, tracks five posture problems with MediaPipe, and offers eight guided stretches with camera-based rep counting. Maizy, an animated companion powered by ElevenLabs, responds to “Hey Maizy,” coaches you through stretches and controls the app by voice. IndyKite filters stretches based on a health check, with the same rules available offline. Pose detection runs on your laptop and no video leaves it; voice interactions use ElevenLabs.
