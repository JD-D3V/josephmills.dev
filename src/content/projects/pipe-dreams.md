Built with one teammate at the IEEE Southern Alberta Industry Hackathon 2026, where it won Best Project Built with ElevenLabs out of 26 teams. Other tools rank water mains; Pipe Dreams audits its own ranking against 37,538 past breaks and 60,740 pipe segments, tests revisions to its plan, and only adopts one that passes a frozen validation gate. A React/TypeScript dashboard sits on a FastAPI backend, and an ElevenLabs voice copilot talks city staff through why each pipe was ranked. The demo video is the last item in the gallery (unmute it for the voice).
